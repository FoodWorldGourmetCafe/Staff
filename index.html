      Staff Manual

      员工店运营流程 · 开店 / 外卖 / 收店

      01

      Opening

      开店流程

      设备开启
      1. 打开 Menulog 机器
      2. 打开 Uber Eats 机器
      3. 打开收款台
      基础准备
      1. 烧水壶添满水
      2. 摆放 sauce 瓶子：Chili Paste & Hoisin Sauce
      3. 检查餐具数量，如不足则补齐
      4. 放菜单到外面
      5. 冰箱取出外卖 sauce
      Sauce 制作
      1. Fish Sauce（罐装 chili + carrot）× 4 盘
      2. XO Sauce（肉 + 油）× 3 盘
      3. Ginger & Green Shallot Sauce × 2 盘
      4. Hainan Sauce（姜 + 罐装 chili）× 3 + Pepper sauce × 3
      5. 做外卖 sauce
      拖地（清洁人员）
      1. 清扫地面杂物
      2. 调配拖地水：Crystal 清洁剂少量 + Jiff 适量
      3. 拖完整个餐厅（前厅、走道、桌底及角落）
      开店前检查
      1. Menulog & Uber 机器正常
      2. 翻牌子（open）并开灯
      3. Sauce 已补齐
      4. 餐具充足
      5. 地面干净
      6. 烧水壶水量足
      7. 桌面整洁
      02

      Takeaway & Delivery

      外卖流程

      接单
      1. 确认订单（顾客备注过敏或注意事项用笔圈出）
      2. 订单为 Collection（自取）圈出
      3. 打印 2 张小票：一张留外场底单，一张夹塑料彩色夹子送入厨房
      打包
      1. 从厨房拿餐时注意冷热分开（热菜一袋，沙拉 / 米卷冷菜一袋）
      2. 所有冷饮单独装袋
      3. 配 sauce
      4. 检查餐具需求
      5. 将热菜、冷菜、饮料袋整理在一起
      6. 订小票

      顺序可按店内习惯。

      交付外卖
      1. 核对订单号码或名字
      2. 确保所有餐食袋交付快递员或自取顾客
      3. 电话订单自取时，核对名字及餐名
      03

      Closing

      收店流程

      8:30 左右（客人优先）
      1. 若有餐具 → 擦干
      2. 用绿色筐子收回 Hoisin & Chili Sauce 放入冰箱
      3. 收回 bench 桌上的 sauce 并用 spray 清洁桌面
      前台区域
      1. Jasmine Tea → 倒入大茶壶，茶叶倒垃圾桶，贴标签放冰箱
      2. Milk Tea → 倒入大茶壶，茶包倒垃圾桶，贴标签放冰箱
      3. Sugar、Lemon Juice、Condensed Milk → 放冰箱
      4. 外场所有 sauce → 放入冰箱
      5. 检查并关闭冰箱灯（共 2 个）
      6. 关闭饮料冰箱灯（按钮在冰箱上方）
      7. 扔掉 Menulog & Uber Eats 外卖订单
      8. 倒垃圾并换垃圾袋
      9. 外场所有杯子 → 洗净或放入 trolley，清洁柜台
      10. 扔掉当天的纸壳
      8:50 左右
      1. 整理所有订单，用皮筋捆好
      2. 根据情况交班
      3. 关闭所有机器
      4. 关空调、关窗，把菜单收回屋内
      5. 检查确认无遗漏 → 关闭所有灯
      6. 翻牌子（close）
      04

      Restocking

      不忙时的补货与清洁

      不忙的情况
      1. 补酱料（酱油、辣椒酱、海鲜酱）
      2. 补饮料
      3. 补酒
      4. 清洗调料框
      5. 补餐具
      01

      Opening

      Opening procedures

      Switch on equipment
      1. Turn on the Menulog terminal.
      2. Turn on the Uber Eats terminal.
      3. Turn on the till.
      Basic preparation
      1. Fill the kettle with water.
      2. Set out the sauce bottles: Chili Paste & Hoisin Sauce.
      3. Check cutlery quantities and replenish if needed.
      4. Put the menus outside.
      5. Take the takeaway sauces out of the fridge.
      Sauce preparation
      1. Fish Sauce (canned chilli + carrot) × 4 trays.
      2. XO Sauce (meat + oil) × 3 trays.
      3. Ginger & Green Shallot Sauce × 2 trays.
      4. Hainan Sauce (ginger + canned chilli) × 3; Pepper sauce × 3.
      5. Prepare takeaway sauces.
      Mopping (cleaning staff)
      1. Sweep up debris from the floor.
      2. Prepare the mop water: a small amount of Crystal cleaner + an appropriate amount of Jiff.
      3. Mop the entire restaurant, including the dining area, walkways, under tables and corners.
      Before opening
      1. Check that the Menulog & Uber terminals are working.
      2. Turn the sign to OPEN and switch on the lights.
      3. Check that sauces are replenished.
      4. Check that there is enough cutlery.
      5. Check that the floor is clean.
      6. Check that the kettle has enough water.
      7. Check that the tables are tidy.
      02

      Takeaway & Delivery

      Takeaway and delivery procedures

      Receiving orders
      1. Check the order. Circle any allergy notes or special requests with a pen.
      2. Circle Collection if the order is for pickup.
      3. Print two receipts: keep one at the front of house and attach the other to a coloured plastic clip for the kitchen.
      Packing
      1. Keep hot and cold food separate when collecting it from the kitchen: hot dishes in one bag, salads / cold rice paper rolls in another.
      2. Pack all cold drinks separately.
      3. Include the sauces.
      4. Check whether cutlery is needed.
      5. Group the hot food, cold food and drink bags together.
      6. Staple the receipt to the bag.

      The order of steps may follow the restaurant’s usual practice.

      Handing over orders
      1. Check the order number or name.
      2. Make sure all food bags are handed to the delivery driver or pickup customer.
      3. For phone-order pickups, confirm the name and dishes ordered.
      03

      Closing

      Closing procedures

      Around 8:30 pm (customers come first)
      1. Dry any cutlery that needs drying.
      2. Collect the Hoisin & Chili Sauce in the green basket and refrigerate.
      3. Collect the sauces from the bench and clean the surface with spray.
      Front counter area
      1. Jasmine Tea: pour into a large teapot, put the tea leaves in the bin, label and refrigerate.
      2. Milk Tea: pour into a large teapot, put the tea bags in the bin, label and refrigerate.
      3. Refrigerate the Sugar, Lemon Juice and Condensed Milk.
      4. Refrigerate all front-of-house sauces.
      5. Check and switch off the two fridge lights.
      6. Switch off the drinks fridge light (the switch is above the fridge).
      7. Discard the Menulog & Uber Eats takeaway orders.
      8. Empty the bins and replace the bin liners.
      9. Wash all front-of-house cups or place them on the trolley; clean the counter.
      10. Discard the cardboard from the day.
      Around 8:50 pm
      1. Organise all order slips and secure them with a rubber band.
      2. Complete the handover as needed.
      3. Switch off all machines.
      4. Switch off the air conditioning, close the windows and bring the menus inside.
      5. Make a final check that nothing has been missed, then switch off all lights.
      6. Turn the sign to CLOSED.
      04

      Restocking

      Restocking and cleaning during quiet periods

      When it is quiet
      1. Top up sauces: soy sauce, chilli sauce and hoisin sauce.
      2. Restock drinks.
      3. Restock alcoholic drinks.
      4. Clean the condiment holders.
      5. Replenish cutlery.
